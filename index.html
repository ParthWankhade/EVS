About Smart Alandi Initiative

The Smart Alandi Carbon Intelligence System is designed to help residents of Alandi monitor and reduce their carbon footprint. Located in the heart of Maharashtra, Alandi is known for its spiritual significance and growing population.

Our AI-powered platform provides intelligent recommendations for sustainable living, with special focus on reducing emissions during festivals and pilgrimages. Join us in building a greener Alandi!

🚌 Promote PMPML buses for eco-friendly transport
🚶 Encourage walking and cycling in the region
🌸 Reduce festival emissions through awareness
